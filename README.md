• The game will load automatically, and you'll be presented with a map of your kingdom featuring various locations such as forests, rivers, and mountains.
• Use the mouse or keyboard controls to move your character around the map and build structures on it. You can construct buildings, roads, bridges, and other infrastructure using resources found throughout the map.
• To battle monsters, click on them with your character's hand icon. The monster's type will determine which actions you can take against it.
